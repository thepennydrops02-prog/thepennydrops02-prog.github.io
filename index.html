Penny Studio

Penny Studio is the small internal tool behind two animated YouTube channels: The Penny Drops (animated money stories) and Kaputalism (animated stories about companies that went kaput).

The tool is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and metadata to YouTube via the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.
