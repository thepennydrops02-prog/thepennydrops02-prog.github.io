Privacy Policy – Penny Studio

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Penny Studio is used only by the owner of The Penny Drops and Kaputalism YouTube channels to manage those channels.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, Penny Studio accesses the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API in order to upload videos, set thumbnails and edit video titles, descriptions and tags.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared. Access tokens are stored securely by the integration provider and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

Penny Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
